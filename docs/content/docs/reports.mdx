Financial statements are computed by BeanDesk in real time directly from the active ledger. The Accounting Period filter in the header applies to all reports, journal postings, and queries. On the trial balance, balance sheet, and income statement, an account row opens the journal for that account.

## Trial Balance

Presents closing balances in a two-column debit and credit format with a collapsible account hierarchy, validating total debit and credit balance equality with real-time account name filtering.

## Balance Sheet

Aggregates assets, liabilities, and equity, verifying that assets equal liabilities plus equity. Unclosed period earnings are automatically summarized into current period results within equity.

## Income Statement

Categorizes operating revenues, costs, and expenses to calculate period net profit, including year-over-year comparative data against the same period of the prior year (e.g. 2026-03 vs 2025-03, 2026-Q2 vs 2025-Q2, 2026 vs 2025; comparative column is omitted when All time is selected).

## Cash Flow Statement

Computed by the direct method, separating net cash flows across operating, investing, and financing activities with year-over-year comparative data (omitted when All time is selected). Non-cash accrual entries are excluded automatically.

Cash flow mappings rely on metadata on Beancount account opening directives (`open`):

```beancount
2020-01-01 open Assets:Bank:Checking USD
  cash: TRUE
2020-01-01 open Income:Service:Consulting USD
  cashflow: "sales"
```

- `cash: TRUE`: Designates the account as a cash or bank asset. If no account is marked, the page says “No cash accounts configured” instead of three zero amounts.
- `cashflow: "<line>"`: Maps the counter-account to a standard cash flow line. Postings without a line mapping are grouped into Unclassified Items.
- `cashflow-in` / `cashflow-out`: Separates line mappings for inflows and outflows on the same account.

Standard line ids, in catalogue order:

Operating:

- In: `sales`, `operating-other-in`
- Out: `purchases`, `wages`, `taxes`, `operating-other-out`

Investing:

- In: `investment-proceeds`, `investment-income`, `asset-disposal`
- Out: `investment-acquire`, `capex`

Financing:

- In: `borrowings`, `capital`
- Out: `debt-principal`, `debt-interest`, `dividends`

Standard ledgers initialized by BeanDesk include baseline cash metadata for checking accounts (`cash: TRUE`) and paid-in capital (`cashflow: "capital"`). Open additional operating accounts from `policies/base/chart-of-accounts.md` as needed.

## Journal & Document Drilldown

The journal offers multi-dimensional filtering across counterparty, narration, accounting element, and tags. The document column opens a dialog with the postings and linked files. A failed load shows an error and a Retry button, not a blank statement.

- Document Linking: A file under `documents/` is automatically linked when its date and account path match a posting, or when the file name contains the transaction's link tag.
- Document Repository: The Documents page centrally indexes all receipts and invoices archived within the active accounting period.

## BQL Query Console

Provides an interactive console for native Beancount Query Language (BQL) statements executed directly by the Fava engine:

- Includes built-in presets for monthly income and expenses, large transactions, and cash account balances.
- Supports execution shortcuts: Cmd + Enter on macOS and Ctrl + Enter on Windows / Linux.
- Supports column sorting and CSV export. The statement is sent unchanged. A result longer than 50,000 rows keeps the first 50,000 rows and reports the total row count.
