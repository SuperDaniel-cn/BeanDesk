Financial statements are computed directly from the connected ledger. The Accounting Period filter applies across all statements, the journal, documents, and BQL queries.

## Trial Balance

Displays debit and credit balances in parallel columns with a collapsible account hierarchy. Filter by account name, and click any row to inspect postings in the journal.

![Trial balance](/images/en/trial-balance.png)

## Balance Sheet

Presents assets, liabilities, and equity, verifying that assets equal liabilities plus equity. Unclosed period earnings are grouped into the equity section.

![Balance sheet](/images/en/balance-sheet.png)

## Income Statement

Aggregates operating revenue, costs, and expenses to calculate net profit, with comparative analysis against prior periods.

![Income statement](/images/en/income-statement.png)

## Cash Flow Statement

Computed via the direct method across operating, investing, and financing activities. Non-cash accrual entries are excluded.

Mark cash attributes on account opening directives:

```beancount
2020-01-01 open Assets:Bank:Checking CNY
  cash: TRUE
2020-01-01 open Income:Services:Delivery CNY
  cashflow: "sales"
```

- `cash: TRUE`: Designates the account as cash or bank balances.
- `cashflow: "<line>"`: Maps the counter-account to a statutory cash flow line.
- `cashflow-in` / `cashflow-out`: Separates inflows and outflows for the same account.

If no cash accounts are configured, the page displays setup guidance. Cash activity without line mapping is grouped under Unclassified Items.

![Cash flow statement](/images/en/cash-flow.png)
