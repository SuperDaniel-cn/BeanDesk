Financial statements are computed by BeanDesk in real time directly from the active ledger. The Accounting Period filter in the header acts as a global slice, coordinating all reports, journal postings, and query scopes. On the trial balance, balance sheet, and income statement, an account row opens the journal for that account.

## Trial Balance

Presents closing balances in a two-column debit and credit format with a collapsible account hierarchy, validating total debit and credit balance equality with real-time account name filtering.

## Balance Sheet

Aggregates assets, liabilities, and equity, verifying that assets equal liabilities plus equity. Unclosed period earnings are automatically summarized into current period results within equity.

## Income Statement

Categorizes operating revenues, costs, and expenses to calculate period net profit, including comparative analysis against the preceding accounting period.

## Cash Flow Statement

Computed via the direct method in pure functions, separating net cash flows across operating, investing, and financing activities. Non-cash accrual entries are excluded automatically.

Cash flow mappings rely on metadata on Beancount account opening directives (`open`):

```beancount
2020-01-01 open Assets:Bank:Checking CNY
  cash: TRUE
2020-01-01 open Income:Services:Delivery CNY
  cashflow: "sales"
```

- `cash: TRUE`: Designates the account as a cash or bank asset. If no account is marked, the page says “No cash accounts configured” instead of three zero totals.
- `cashflow: "<line>"`: Maps the counter-account to a statutory cash flow line. Postings without a line mapping are grouped into Unclassified Items.
- `cashflow-in` / `cashflow-out`: Separates line mappings for inflows and outflows on the same account.

Standard ledgers initialized by BeanDesk include preconfigured cash metadata for sample bank, revenue, expense, equity, and owner-advance accounts.

## Journal & Document Drilldown

The journal offers multi-dimensional filtering across counterparty, narration, accounting element, and tags. The document column opens a dialog with the postings and linked files. A failed load shows an error and a retry, not an empty book.

- Document Linking: A file under `documents/` is automatically linked when its date and account path match a posting, or when the file name contains the transaction's unique link token.
- Document Repository: The Documents page centrally indexes all receipts and invoices archived within the active accounting period.

## BQL Query Console

Provides an interactive console for native Beancount Query Language (BQL) statements executed directly by the Fava engine:

- Includes built-in presets for monthly income and expenses, large transactions, and cash account balances.
- Supports execution shortcuts: Cmd + Enter on macOS and Ctrl + Enter on Windows / Linux.
- Supports column sorting and CSV export. The statement is sent unchanged. A result longer than 50,000 rows keeps the first 50,000 and says how many came back.
